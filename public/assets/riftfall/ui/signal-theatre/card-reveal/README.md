# Signal Theatre card-reveal assets

These original Ashenreach assets support the Reliquary Aperture host-TV reveal.

- `reliquary-aperture.webp`: 1600×900 environment plate with a central UI-safe aperture.
- `reliquary-card-back.webp`: 720×1080 portrait card back.

Both were generated specifically for Ashenreach, contain no text or logos, and
were optimized to WebP before registration. They are TV-only presentation
assets and are intentionally excluded from the phone derivative pipeline.

Generation direction:

- Chamber: wide 16:9 original mechanical Reliquary Aperture chamber, central
  dark card opening, side rails, worn brass, cyan signal tubes, restrained red
  illumination; no card, character, text, logo, skull, or franchise identity.
- Card back: portrait 2:3 Ashenreach industrial reliquary card back,
  symmetrical blackened iron, worn brass edge rails, cracked ceramic inlays,
  central unlit diamond aperture, dim cyan and red signal light; no text, logo,
  skull, or franchise identity.
