# Battle UX specification

## Information order

1. Who is fighting.
2. Tested skill.
3. Operative and enemy rolls.
4. Public modifiers.
5. Final totals.
6. Winner/tie.
7. Exact consequence or reward.

## TV chamber

- Board recedes; operative and enemy art oppose each other.
- Do not render a separate encounter header or duplicate player/location panel.
- Present both calculations simultaneously: `base + die + modifiers = total`.
- Result appears once, followed by Wound, Scar, recall, Trophy, or reward.
- Reconnect displays the authoritative current stage and does not replay completed dice/outcome motion.

## Phone

- Owned battle actions replace general tabs: assistance, valid equipment, roll, reroll/reaction, accept/continue.
- Enemy die is automatic and hidden until the fair reveal point.
- Other players receive named waiting state, not controls.

## Motion

Entry 360–520 ms; dice 520–760 ms; calculation 220 ms; result 360 ms; return 360 ms. Reduced motion uses fade and stable totals.
