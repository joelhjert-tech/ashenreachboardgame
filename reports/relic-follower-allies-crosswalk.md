# Relic Allies / Ashen Reach Followers Crosswalk

## Confirmed Relic structure

The base Relic rulebook treats Allies as Asset cards embedded in the colored Threat decks rather than as a separate recruitment deck. Exploration randomly reveals Threat cards; after Events, Enemies, and Encounters are resolved, surviving players acquire Asset cards on the space. Assets remain face-up, use their printed timing, share the normal Asset holding limit, and may carry charges. Vanquished or corrupted characters retain Assets, while complete player elimination discards them. The Nemesis expansion retains the `Asset — Ally` structure.

Sources reviewed:

- Fantasy Flight Games, *Relic Rulebook*, Threat and Asset sections: https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/73/3c/733c3e1d-ffd0-4cac-a9ef-08783b367f99/re01_relic_rulebook.pdf
- Repository Relic mission research and card archive. Mission cards were used only as structural research; no names, card text, artwork, or proprietary terminology were copied.

No general Ally trading rule or general starting-Ally rule was confirmed in the reviewed base rules. Those are therefore not treated as Relic requirements.

## Inferred design principles

- Companions should enter play through adventure resolution rather than a catalog/debug surface.
- Acquisition should follow meaningful risk and create a visible progression moment.
- Strong companions should be rarer than normal items.
- Printed timing and holding pressure prevent Allies from becoming an undifferentiated bonus pile.
- Ownership and loss must be predictable when a character is defeated or replaced.

## Crosswalk

| System | Relic structure | Ashen Reach implemented structure | Intentional difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discovery | Random Asset cards in Threat decks | Fixed lore-matched board rewards plus rare random Artifact companions | More authored location identity |
| Acquisition | Automatic after the encounter is survived | Automatic authoritative `gain_follower` after a successful source | No recruitment confirmation tax |
| Selection | Primarily random card discovery | Fixed standard followers; server-random rare companion Artifact | Strong companions stay surprising |
| Ownership | Face-up Asset in owner play area | Stable-ID follower attached to operative | Full rule and notes stay owner-private |
| Public state | Asset is visible | Name, tier, count, and generic activity are public-safe | Private route/contract notes remain phone-only |
| Capacity | Shared Asset limit | No generic follower limit | Sparse sources and duplicate rules provide sufficient pressure |
| Duplicates | Governed by physical deck copies | One stable ID per operative; unique followers once per table | Server validates identity directly |
| Use | Printed passive/activated timing | Explicit passive, active, or reaction ability; owner-only server request | No role fallback |
| Loss | Voluntary/excess discard and elimination | Explicit discard-use only; recall retains; character replacement and New Game clear | No generic follower trading or capacity discard |
| Persistence | Survives normal character setbacks | Survives Wounds, battle loss, recall, save, and reconnect | Replacement begins with the new template |

Ashen Reach retains the canonical term **Follower**. The final structure delivers the same adventure-discovery and character-development value without cloning Relic terminology or content.
