# Mission Reward Balance Review

## Current reward population

There are 36 Missions. After normalization, typed effect totals are:

| Effect | Count |
|---|---:|
| Normal Equipment grant | 12 |
| Salvage gain | 8 |
| Trophy gain | 5 |
| Heal Wound | 2 |
| Follower gain | 2 |
| Display-only completion text | 16 |
| Direct Artifact grant | 0 |

Effects may coexist on a Mission, so totals do not sum to 36.

## Tier review

- The 16 former note-only Missions grant only their Completed Mission credential. This is mechanically modest and should be observed in full-session playtests; no compensating value was invented during schema cleanup.
- Eight Salvage rewards are all one Salvage. No routine Mission was increased to two.
- Trophy rewards remain progression-only and do not substitute for Salvage.
- Normal Equipment rewards are valuable but no longer bypass Artifact rarity.
- Followers remain rare at two Mission sources.

## Confirmed correction

Ten ordinary Missions granted Artifact-tier gear. This made one Mission worth both an Artifact and one-third of the next Artifact requisition. Those rewards were replaced with normal Equipment of matching fiction and slot role. This is a correctness/economy-boundary repair, not a speculative global rebalance.

## Deferred playtest questions

1. Do note-only Missions feel sufficiently rewarding when their credential is the only mechanical payout?
2. Are twelve normal Equipment grants too inventory-heavy after auto-equip and capacity rules?
3. Does the first three-Mission requisition occur in late midgame during real sessions?
4. Are Trophy Mission rewards competitive with Salvage and Equipment rewards?
5. Should difficult multi-stop Missions receive a second modest typed effect after playtest evidence?

No starting Salvage, prices, Trophy thresholds, Mission requirement, Artifact availability, or scenario values changed.
