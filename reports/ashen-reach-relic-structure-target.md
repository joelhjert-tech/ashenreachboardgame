# Ashen Reach Relic-Structure Target

## Decision status

This document defines Phase A only: quantitative structure, category mapping, and evidence gates. It does not authorize new cards, schema expansion, retirement, balance changes, or direct use of Relic text/art.

## Exact Relic template

The threat archive contains 261 category members and 261 original scans.

Wiki category metadata identifies 12 Relic scenario sheets. It exposes no separate “Challenge Cards” category. For this design mapping, the 47 cards categorized as Encounters are the only defensible challenge-style population. This is an explicit structural proxy, not a claim that Relic names them challenge cards.

No mixed enemy/hazard category is present in the API metadata. Boss/mini-boss, persistence, choice, multiplayer, reward, and failure subtypes are not exhaustively encoded. They remain unapproved numerical targets until the scans receive a card-by-card mechanical transcription.

## Evidence gate for distributions not yet exact

The manifest intentionally marks detailed timing, reward, failure, movement, equipment, choice, persistence, and multiplayer fields uncertain. Therefore Phase A does **not** invent exact counts for:

- damage/Wound frequency;
- resource loss or reward frequency;
- movement effects;
- equipment interaction;
- choice or group effects;
- boss/elite status;
- persistent duration;
- full success/failure asymmetry.

Before those become validation targets, a follow-up private transcription must classify all 261 scans using paraphrases only, record reviewer confidence, and reconcile totals. No OCR dump or copied rules text may be committed.

## Ashen Reach difficulty bands

Current bands are retained as the translation scale:

- Low: difficulty 2–4.
- Standard: 5–7.
- High: 8–9.
- Elite: 10–12.

Relic’s value curve is heavily low/mid with a thin elite tail. Ashen Reach should preserve that shape per lane, not copy printed values. New cards should target roughly half standard, one quarter low, one fifth high, and a small elite tail until the scan-level distribution is formally translated.

## Copyright and authority boundary

All future content must use original IDs, names, lore, effects, and art. Relic files remain ignored, private, and outside runtime roots. Historical saved Heat is never converted. New content uses Grit, Forge, Guile, Signal, Command, Wounds, Scars, Salvage, typed movement, exact-instance equipment state, and server-authored source IDs.

## Phase exit criteria

Phase A is complete when these four reports are approved. No target-count validator should be enabled until each content family has approved IDs and typed roles; otherwise current valid content would be made intentionally invalid for several phases.
