## Coverage gaps

- No verified universal carry limit, follower limit, trade action, general PvP attack, or alignment system.
- Reroll-of-reroll and equal-priority replacement ordering lack a single general policy.
- Heat persists in schema/content compatibility and old docs despite Scars being the intended player-facing successor.
- Reconnection is strongly tested at transport/state level, but not every pending reaction has a dedicated end-to-end browser test.
- Some content pools are validated and projected more broadly than their complete runtime resolution coverage.
